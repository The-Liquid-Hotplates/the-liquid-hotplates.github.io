Auditions

This is the general process of auditions for LHP! We usually host auditions in the beginning of fall quarter every year, and sometimes at the beginning of other quarters depending on need!
No experience needed! Just bring yourself :]

Please reach out to us if you have any questions regarding the process.

01

General Auditions

Towards the beginning of fall, all groups host a joint audition that usually spans a couple hours in the evening/night for two days! Locations and times will be posted all UC Davis Acapella groups' Instagrams.

All you have to do is walk-in to the audition room listed on the flyer, and you will be taken care of! You will not have to be there the entire time - once you are done with your audition, you are free to go!

What you need to prepare: A 30-60 second solo song of your choice!
What you will be asked: Basic introduction, singing scales, pitch recall, & singing your solo song!

If you have been invited back to callbacks, groups will email you around midnight of the second night of auditions!

**If you are unable to make both days, please let groups know AHEAD OF TIME so they may be able to accomodate you.

02

Callbacks

If you were invited back to LHP Callbacks, all details will be listed in the email invite!
