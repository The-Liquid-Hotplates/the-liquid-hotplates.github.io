Who We Are

Established in 2000, The Liquid Hotplates is University of California, Davis's hottest all-gender a cappella group. Every year, we compete at ICCAs (International Championship of Collegiate A Cappella) & most recently placed 3rd at ICCA West Semifinals in which we recieved awards for Outstanding Vocal Percussion & Outstanding Soloists. We also host two of our own showcases - Alottacappella in the fall, and Off The Record in the spring! Make sure to keep an eye out for those through our Instagram!

Follow our Instagram!

Every February, we compete at ICCAs (International Championship of Collegiate A Cappella) hosted by Varsity Vocals - one of our biggest events of the year! LHP has brought home many different awards throughout the years, including our recent 1st place finish at ICCA West Quarterfinals 2026! You can watch our ICCA sets on YouTube!

Contact Us

Every new member joins as a "babyplate" - our name for the newest voices still finding their footing in the group. From late-night rehearsal runs to our annual group photoshoot, LHP is as much about the friendships as it is about the music. Follow along on Instagram to see what we're up to between shows.
