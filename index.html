The Liquid Hotplates

The HOTTEST A Cappella group at UC Davis

Who We Are

Established in 2000, The Liquid Hotplates is University of California, Davis's hottest all-gender a cappella group.

Every year, we compete at ICCAs (International Championship of Collegiate A Cappella) & most recently placed 1st at ICCA West Quarterfinals before going on to place 3rd at ICCA West Semifinals, where we recieved awards for Outstanding Vocal Percussion & Outstanding Soloists.

We also host two of our own showcases - Alottacappella in the fall, and Off The Record in the spring! Make sure to keep an eye out for those through our Instagram!

Follow our Instagram!

Every February, we compete at ICCAs (International Championship of Collegiate A Cappella) hosted by Varsity Vocals - one of our biggest events of the year! LHP has brought home many different awards throughout the years, including our recent 1st place win at ICCA West Quarterfinals 2026! You can watch our ICCA sets on YouTube!

We record our original arrangements some competition seasons. Check out our latest releases on Spotify & YouTube, with more on the way!

Listen to Us!

We perform student made arrangements including genres such as pop, alternative, and RnB. You can find us at different events on campus, & throughout California - including our own two annual showcases, Alottacappella and Off The Record. If you're interested in booking LHP for an event, reach out via our Instagram or email!

Contact Us

Every year, we welcome new memebers into our group as our "babyplates" - and within the first few weeks, they are already impossible to imagine LHP without. From late-night rehearsals and dinners to casual studying on campus, LHP is just as much about the friendships and community as it is about the music. Follow along on Instagram to see what we're up to together throughout the year.
